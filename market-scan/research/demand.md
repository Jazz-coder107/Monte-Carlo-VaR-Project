## FINDING 1 — Upwork: overall AI skill demand more than doubled

- **Claim:** Demand for the top AI-specific skills on Upwork grew 109% year over year in 2025 (measured by freelancer earnings on completed jobs, US-originated demand, Jan 1–Dec 31 2025), vs 23% growth for other high-demand skills.
- **Evidence (search snippet):** "The top skills explicitly tied to applying AI within existing roles grew 109% year over year." / "The demand for AI-related skills... more than doubled in the last year, compared to 23% growth for other high-demand skills." / Methodology: "Skills data was sourced from the Upwork Marketplace and is based on freelancer earnings across six work categories from January 1, 2025, to December 31, 2025, with demand originating in the United States. To ensure a strong signal of demand, only completed jobs are analyzed."
- **Source:** https://www.upwork.com/press/releases/upworks-in-demand-skills-2026-demand-for-top-ai-skills-more-than-doubles-as-ai-is-embedded-into-everyday-work (press release dated 2026-02-04; same text mirrored at investors.upwork.com and globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/02/04/3232122; CNBC coverage: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/02/09/upwork-fastest-growing-in-demand-skills-companies-are-hiring-for.html)
- **Tier:** 1 (platform's own annual In-Demand Skills report)
- **Origin:** Upwork internal marketplace data.
- **Confidence:** established-leaning probable (figure appeared identically in 5+ independent search result sets; pages not opened directly).

- **Evidence (search snippets):** "Q2 2026 revenue was $97.8 million, down 10% year-over-year, primarily due to a decline in low-value transactional work." / "ongoing compression in high-volume, low-value transactional work due to rapid AI adoption, while high-value projects grew to 15% of gross order value." / "13% year-over-year growth in clients completing $1,000+ projects on a trailing twelve-month basis." / "significant increases in demand for AI-related services such as AI user-generated content video ads and AI mobile app development."

## FINDING 9 — Writing/translation demand shrank — verified across independent sources

- **Claim:** Writing and translation are the clearest shrinking categories: writing job postings on Upwork fell ~30-33% and translation ~19% after ChatGPT's mainstreaming, with academic estimates of a 20-50% decline in demand for AI-substitutable skills; declines continued into 2025 (writing projects reported -32% YoY, the largest category drop on Upwork).
- **Evidence (search snippets):** Bloomberry: "Writing jobs declined 33%, translation jobs declined 19%, and customer service jobs declined 16%. The hourly rates for translation and writing jobs also declined ~20% and ~4% respectively" (Upwork postings, Nov 1 2022–Feb 14 2024). / Academic: "Demand for substitutable skills, such as writing and translation, decreased by 20–50% relative to the counterfactual trend, with the sharpest decline observed for short-term (1-3 week) jobs." / Imperial/Harvard/DIW study of ~2M postings: "within eight months of ChatGPT's launch, demand for freelance writing jobs fell roughly 30%, the steepest decline of any category studied." / Mediabistro (2026): "Writing projects on Upwork declined 32% year over year in 2025, the largest drop of any category on the platform. Translation jobs declined 19%."
- **Sources:** https://bloomberry.com/blog/i-analyzed-5m-freelancing-jobs-to-see-what-jobs-are-being-replaced-by-ai/ (Bloomberry, ~April 2024; Entrepreneur coverage https://www.entrepreneur.com/business-news/ai-is-taking-over-these-freelancing-jobs-the-most-report/471183) ; "Winners and losers of generative AI: Early Evidence of Shifts in Freelancer Demand" https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0167268124004591 (J. Economic Behavior & Organization, 2024) ; https://www.mediabistro.com/go-freelance/freelance-writing-jobs-in-the-age-of-ai-what-the-data-says-and-how-to-position-yourself/ (2026)
- **Tier:** 2 (independent scraped-data analysis + peer-reviewed research); mediabistro tier 3.
- **Origin:** Bloomberry = own scrape of Upwork postings; ScienceDirect = academic analysis of freelance platform data; mediabistro's -32%/-19% figures may partially recycle Bloomberry — origin not fully disambiguated (flagged).
- **Confidence:** established for the 2023-24 decline (multiple independent methodologies converge); probable for continued 2025 decline. Note the tension with Upwork's own PR claim (Finding 3) that demand "remained strong" in vulnerable categories — platform PR vs third-party posting counts diverge.

## RANKED ANSWER (synthesis)

Highest-demand AI categories on freelance marketplaces, mid-2026, by convergence of evidence:

1. **AI video generation & editing** — top growth on BOTH Upwork (+329%) and Fiverr (Video & Animation +278%; "AI video creators" searches +66%/6mo). Strongest cross-platform signal.
2. **AI integration / automation engineering** (connecting AI into workflows: n8n, Claude Code, agent builds) — Upwork "AI integration" +178%; Fiverr Claude Code +938%, n8n +125%; live posting language centers on n8n/LangChain/LangGraph/RAG/MCP.
3. **AI strategy & consulting** — fastest-growing AI GSV sub-category in Upwork's earnings (+50% YoY Q2 2026).
4. **AI data annotation & labeling** — Upwork +154%.
5. **AI agent / chatbot / voice-agent development** — Upwork chatbot dev +71%; Fiverr AI voice agents +49%; Freelancer.com strategic pivot to agent development.
6. Shrinking: **writing (-30-33%), translation (-19%), customer service (-16%)** posting volumes; low-value transactional gigs generally (visible in Fiverr's -10% revenue).

## SURPRISES (contradicted expectations)

1. **Creative, not coding, leads AI demand growth** — AI video tops both platforms; Fiverr's Data category grew only +3% while Video & Animation grew +278%.
2. **Fiverr's aggregate business is shrinking double-digits** (-10% Q2, FY guide -14 to -17%) even as its AI categories boom — AI demand is a rotation within a compressing low-end market, not a rising tide.
3. **Upwork's AI GSV growth is decelerating** (>50% YoY Q4'25 → >40% Q1'26 → >22% Q2'26) despite the hype cycle.
4. **Prompt engineering** — the emblematic "AI job of 2023" — collapsed as a standalone title while surviving as an embedded skill.
5. **Platform PR vs independent data tension:** Upwork's report says demand "remained consistently strong" in automation-vulnerable categories, while independent posting-count analyses (Bloomberry, peer-reviewed studies) show 20-50% declines in writing/translation — both can be true (earnings-weighted vs posting-count metrics) but the framing gap is notable.
